# What we can say (facts and rules)

Audience: **benefits brokers and advisors** in Canada. Secondary: HR at their client companies.
Goal: brokers see BenefitTrack as a way to show value and keep clients at renewal.

## Approved facts (from BenefitTrack.ca)
- Hero line: "Make benefits easier to understand and use."
- How it works, 3 steps: Drop in your booklet / Choose what matters / Get a useful reminder.
- Step detail: see what the plan covers in plain language; limits and reset dates;
  connect a supported carrier for current balances; reminders by email at the frequency the employee chooses,
  with the next step toward care.
- "Benefit categories reset on different schedules, so a single year-end reminder isn't enough."
- Not another dashboard: summaries and reminders arrive by email.
- Reminder email subject: "Your health benefit checkup is here".
- Free for employees. Data never sold. PIPEDA compliant. Built in Canada. The original booklet is not stored.
- Partners: employers, TPAs (third-party administrators) and benefits advisors.
- Founder quote (exact wording only): "I realized I had $3,200 worth of massages, physio, and therapy
  appointments just sitting there." Attribute to "BenefitTrack's founder". Do not use "he" or "she".

## Hard rules
1. **No invented numbers, stats, studies, customers, testimonials or reviews.** The only real figure is the
   founder's $3,200. Example balances inside a product mock-up ("$500 left") are fine because they are
   clearly illustrative screens, never presented as data.
2. No claims beyond the facts above (no "most employees", "nobody reads", "saves X%").
   Soften with "can", "may", "easy to miss".
3. Never name competitors, carriers or real companies.
4. No medical, legal or financial advice.
5. Never show or name real people other than the founder quote above. No photos of people.
6. Captions: hook in the first line (under about 125 characters), 2 to 4 short paragraphs, one call to action
   (BenefitTrack.ca / link in bio), then exactly 5 hashtags. Bullets, when used, are "•".
7. Hashtags: pick 5 from
   #employeebenefits #groupbenefits #benefitsbroker #healthbenefits #benefitsadvisor #insurancebroker
   #clientretention #renewalseason #employeeengagement #paramedical #HRcanada #clientexperience #BenefitTrack
8. Every image needs alt text describing the slide in one sentence.
9. Do not repeat a topic used in the last 6 weeks (check `log.json` at the repository root).
